## Full Description

18 Sitters helps families and caregivers connect through a refined community-focused care experience. Discover caregivers, review profiles, manage bookings, track sessions, message contacts, and build trusted relationships around shared values.

Designed for a calm, premium experience, 18 Sitters brings family care, caregiver profiles, referrals, rewards, and subscription management into one simple app.
