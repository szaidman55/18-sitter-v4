# 18 Sitters Launch Marketing Plan

## Positioning

18 Sitters is the premium babysitting app for Jewish families and caregivers who want trusted care, shared values, and a calmer way to connect.

Core line:
Trusted care. Jewish values.

Launch promise:
Find caregivers who understand your home, your calendar, your customs, and your kids.

Do not lead with:
- Generic babysitting app language.
- Overly religious symbolism.
- Claims like background checked, fully vetted, insured, or guaranteed unless those operational processes are live.

Lead with:
- Jewish community.
- Shared values.
- Profiles, response rates, reviews, filters, bookings, messaging, and session history.
- Calm, premium, parent-first ease.

## Audiences

Primary family audience:
Jewish parents who need reliable sitters for date nights, Shabbat, holidays, after-school help, Hebrew-friendly homes, kosher homes, and community referrals.

Caregiver audience:
Babysitters and nannies who want to be found by families who value their experience, languages, cultural familiarity, reliability, and care.

Secondary audience:
Community connectors, synagogue groups, school parent groups, local Jewish organizations, and family WhatsApp groups.

## Launch Content Pillars

1. The Friday problem
Parents need trusted help before Shabbat, date night, or a community event.

2. Shared values
Families want someone who understands the home, not just the job.

3. Better discovery
Filters for Shabbat, kosher home, Hebrew, French, overnight, nearby, infant care, tutoring, and experience.

4. Trust signals
Profiles, response rate, reviews, ratings, messaging, bookings, and session history.

5. Caregiver growth
Caregivers can build a profile, receive requests, track sessions, and grow through referrals.

6. Community referral loop
The app grows because families invite families and caregivers invite caregivers.

## Launch Cadence

Pre-launch, 7 days:
- Day 1: Teaser Reel/TikTok, "The sitter search is getting calmer."
- Day 2: Parent pain point video, "Friday at 3 PM."
- Day 3: Caregiver recruitment video.
- Day 4: Product preview, quick app screen flow.
- Day 5: Community values post.
- Day 6: Founder or voiceover launch story.
- Day 7: Waitlist/referral CTA.

Launch week:
- Day 1: Launch announcement across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts.
- Day 2: Feature video, "Find care near you."
- Day 3: Caregiver profile spotlight.
- Day 4: Referral/rewards post.
- Day 5: YouTube 60-90 second launch video.
- Day 6: UGC prompt, "What is your hardest sitter moment?"
- Day 7: Community partner post for schools/synagogues/groups.

Post-launch, weeks 2-4:
- 3 short videos per week.
- 1 caregiver recruitment video per week.
- 1 parent testimonial or scripted testimonial per week.
- 1 YouTube Short per week.
- 1 longer YouTube demo or founder update every 2 weeks.

## Visual Direction

Format:
- 9:16 for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts.
- 16:9 for YouTube launch/demo video.

Look:
- Ivory background, navy typography, warm gold accents.
- App screen recordings framed cleanly, not crowded.
- Calm homes, table details, calendar, phone in hand, parent getting ready, caregiver arriving, kids activity.
- Premium family concierge feeling.

Avoid:
- Cartoon childcare visuals.
- Loud influencer gimmicks.
- Overly religious iconography.
- Busy stock footage.
- Scary fear-based parent messaging.

## Measurement

Track weekly:
- Video views by platform.
- 3-second hold rate.
- Average watch time.
- Saves and shares.
- Profile visits.
- Link clicks.
- Waitlist signups or app installs.
- Family signups vs caregiver signups.
- Referral code usage.

Winning content signal:
High saves and shares from parents matter more than raw views. The product is community-based, so forwarded content is the growth engine.
