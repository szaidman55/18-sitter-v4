Title:
Introducing 18 Sitters

Visual: Quiet home, parent glancing at calendar, phone with unanswered sitter texts.
Voiceover:
Every family has had the moment. Plans are set, the kids are ready, and the sitter search suddenly becomes the hardest part of the night.

Problem, 10-25 seconds:
Visual: Group chats, notes app names, parent switching between messages.
Voiceover:
For Jewish families, care can come with extra context: Shabbat timing, kosher homes, Hebrew, holidays, community trust, and the feeling that someone understands the home they are entering.

Solution, 25-50 seconds:
Visual: 18 Sitters app. Find Care, filters, profile, reviews, response rate, messages.

18 Sitters brings that search into one calm, refined place. Discover caregivers nearby. Review profiles, skills, languages, response rates, and reviews. Message, book, and keep a record of sessions.

Close, 78-90 seconds:
