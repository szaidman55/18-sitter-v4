# Video Production Checklist

## Assets To Prepare

- 18 Sitters logo on ivory background.
- 18 Sitters logo on navy background.
- App icon.
- Screen recordings:
  - Splash screen.
  - Role selection.
  - Find Care.
  - Filters.
  - Caregiver profile.
  - Messages.
  - Bookings.
  - Session log.
  - Referral screen.
  - Caregiver dashboard.
- 3-5 lifestyle clips:
  - Parent checking calendar.
  - Phone in hand.
  - Caregiver arriving at door.
  - Kids activity at table.
  - Parent leaving for dinner or event.

## Screen Recording Notes

- Use 9:16 mobile recordings for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts.
- Keep text labels large and high contrast.
- Use slow, deliberate taps.
- Remove any prototype-only navigation before public capture.
- Avoid showing personal contact details unless they are fictional.
- Use demo names only.

## Editing Style

- Pace: calm but modern.
- Cuts: 1.5-3 seconds per shot.
- Music: warm, minimal, refined, not childish.
- Typography: navy or ivory, gold accents only.
- End card: logo, tagline, CTA.

## Export Settings

Instagram Reels:
- 1080 x 1920
- 9:16
- 24 or 30 fps
- MP4 H.264
- Keep under 60 seconds

TikTok:
- 1080 x 1920
- 9:16
- 24 or 30 fps
- MP4 H.264
- Hook in first 1.5 seconds

YouTube Shorts:
- 1080 x 1920
- 9:16
- Under 60 seconds
- Title with clear search terms

YouTube Launch Video:
- 1920 x 1080
- 16:9
- 60-90 seconds
- Include captions

## Launch Posting Checklist

- Bio link points to waitlist, app store page, or landing page.
- Profile image uses icon-only logo.
- First pinned post: Launch announcement.
- Second pinned post: How it works.
- Third pinned post: Caregiver recruitment.
- Captions include family and caregiver CTA.
- Comments monitored for caregiver questions, launch city questions, pricing, safety, and referral details.

## Comment Reply Bank

Question: Where is this available?
Reply: We are launching community by community. Join the launch list and tell us your city so we can prioritize your area.

Question: Can caregivers join now?
Reply: Yes. Caregivers can create a profile for families to discover as the community launches.

Question: Is this only for Jewish families?
Reply: 18 Sitters is designed around Jewish community care and shared values. Families and caregivers who connect with that context are welcome.

Question: Are payments handled in the app?
Reply: Sessions can be logged in the app. Payments happen directly between families and caregivers unless a future release adds payment processing.

Question: What makes this different from a regular babysitting app?
Reply: 18 Sitters is built around Jewish community context, including profiles, values-based filters, response signals, messaging, bookings, session history, and referrals.
