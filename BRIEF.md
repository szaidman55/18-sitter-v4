18 Sitters
Designer Brief & Brand Identity Instructions
Based on the approved visual reference
Approved reference direction: refined navy-and-gold 18 monogram with protective gold arc.
1. Project
Create a polished, professional vector logo and basic brand identity for 18 Sitters, a babysitting app for the Jewish
community.
The final design must be based on the attached reference image. Preserve this exact visual direction: elegant, premium,
calm, intelligent, modern, and community-based.
This should not look childish, cartoonish, daycare-like, or overly playful.

2. Brand Name
Brand name: 18 Sitters
The number 18 refers subtly to chai, meaning life. This meaning should be present in the concept, but the logo should
remain elegant and understated.
Do not add Hebrew letters or a visible chai symbol inside the main logo.
3. Brand Personality
The brand should feel:
• Premium
• Trustworthy
• Calm
• Intelligent
• Warm
• Refined
• Community-focused
• Family-oriented
• Modern
The brand should not feel:
• Childish
• Cartoonish
• Cheap
• Generic
• Overly religious
• Too playful
• Like a daycare or toy brand
The target feeling is closer to a premium family concierge service or private trusted community platform, not a typical
babysitting app.
4. Main Logo Direction
The main logo should be a refined 18 monogram with the wordmark underneath. Use the reference image as the main
direction.
The logo should contain:
• A large elegant 18
• A thin warm gold arc wrapping around the 18
• The wordmark 18 SITTERS
• Optional tagline: TRUSTED CARE. JEWISH VALUES.
The design should be clean, spacious, and balanced.
5. Exact Fonts
Large 18 monogram
Use Canela Regular for the large “18” icon.
The 18 should feel like a luxury monogram: elegant, high-contrast, refined, and timeless.
Wordmark
Use Söhne Medium for:

Tagline

6. Logo Proportions
The 1 and 8 in the large monogram must be optically balanced.
The “1” should not appear much taller than the “8.”
Guideline:
Top of 1 ≈ Top of 8
Bottom of 1 ≈ Bottom of 8
This does not need to be mathematically identical, but it must look visually balanced. The 1 can keep its elegant serif detail,
but it should not dominate the 8.
7. Gold Arc
Add a thin warm gold arc around the right side of the 18, similar to the reference.
The arc should suggest protection, care, community, trust, and continuity.
It should be subtle and elegant.
Important rules:
• Do not make the arc too thick.
• Do not make it look like a parent/person.
• Do not add a circle/head on top.
• Do not make it look like a childcare icon.
• Do not make it too decorative.
• Keep it refined, simple, and premium.
The arc should wrap partly around the 18, mostly on the right side, with a light crescent feeling.

13. Jewish Symbolism
The main logo should not include a Star of David, Hebrew letters, menorah, chai symbol, or any obvious religious icon.

The Jewish identity should be subtle and dignified.
It can appear in secondary brand materials, for example:
• Small line icon of a shield with subtle Star of David
• Community icon
• Values-based messaging
• Brand book explanations
But the core logo itself should remain clean and premium.

14. Visual Style
The final look should match the attached reference:
• Elegant white/ivory background
• Navy and gold palette
• Lots of whitespace
• Premium editorial typography
• Refined monogram
• Subtle gold arc
• Clean wordmark
• Minimal explanations/icons in the brand presentation
The design should feel suitable for an app, a website, an investor presentation, a community launch, and a premium family
service.

The logo should always have generous breathing room and should never feel cramped.

20. Final Design Goal
The final logo should feel like this:
A refined navy-and-gold luxury monogram for a trusted Jewish community babysitting app.
